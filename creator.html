Build the Future Together

Are you a creator on Instagram or TikTok? Do you love productivity hacks, cutting-edge AI, and beautifully designed native desktop experiences? Let's build the future of desktop workflow automation together.

We are seeking highly engaged visual storytellers to join the Glide Creator Program and show the world how personal desktop agents are changing how we work, dictate, and build.

What We Offer

  • Premium Compensation: We pay anywhere from $20 to $1,000 per video. The exact rate is flexible and scales based on your audience size, video production value, and historical engagement metrics.
  • Long-Term Partnerships: We are looking to cultivate true, multi-month brand ambassadors. For creators who align deeply with Glide's vision, we offer partnerships that involve equity ownership in the company.
  • First-Access Tools: Get early beta access to new features, system agent workflows, and direct access to the design team.

Requirements

  • Active Audiences: Creators on Instagram Reels and TikTok.
  • High Engagement: We prioritize creators who have exceptional engagement rates and a track record of videos with high view counts. We seek genuine viewer connection over empty follower counts.
  • Focus Areas: Tech tutorials, developer setups, design workflows, lifehacks, macOS customization, or productivity tips.
