Two kinds of app

Dictation apps such as Wispr Flow and Superwhisper turn your voice into text wherever you type. Screen assistants such as Clicky look at your screen when you ask, talk back and do tasks. Lemon sits between the two: it turns speech into prompts and messages. Glide does both jobs: hold Fn to dictate, or hold Control and Option to ask the assistant about what is on your screen.
