Glide vs Clicky

These two are the closest pair: both look at your screen when you press a key, answer out loud, point at things and run agents. The differences are in dictation, privacy and breadth.

Clicky facts checked October 18, 2026 on Clicky's own pages. Sources are at the end.

The short answer

Choose Glide if you want dictation that runs on your Mac and is unlimited on every plan, and an assistant whose requests are answered and discarded rather than kept.

Choose Clicky if you want the widest set of agent connectors today, agents that run on a schedule, drawing on screen, or more languages.

Where Glide is different

  • Dictation on your Mac, without a cap. Glide’s speech-to-text runs locally and is unlimited on every plan. Clicky processes everything in the cloud.
  • Less kept about you. Glide does not store your prompts, chat history or screen contents on its servers. Clicky keeps the AI’s analysis of each screenshot and your prompt.
  • It asks first. Before an agent sends anything on your behalf, Glide asks you.

Where Clicky is stronger

  • More connectors: Gmail, Drive, Sheets, Notion, Linear, Slack, Stripe and more, several accounts per app, and your own MCP connections.
  • Routines: agents with memory and files that run on a schedule.
  • Drawing and languages: hand-drawn annotations on screen, 70+ languages for voice and 50+ for dictation.

Switching from Clicky

The talk key is the same, Control and Option, so the habit carries over. Dictation moves to Fn alone.

  • If macOS has Fn set to show emoji or start Apple’s dictation, set Press fn key to to Do Nothing in Keyboard settings.
  • Run one assistant at a time, since both listen for Control and Option.
