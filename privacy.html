Privacy Policy

Your data stays under your control. Learn about how we process transient payloads securely.

At Glide, we prioritize your privacy and the protection of your data. We are committed to safeguarding all data collected, both personal and non-personal, adhering to the highest global standards of data protection. Our policies are designed to keep your personal data under your control while maintaining compliance with relevant frameworks, including GDPR.

1. Notice

Whenever we collect information that could be used to personally identify you, we will explicitly ask for your provision and permission. This policy ensures that you are fully informed and consenting to the collection of your personal data.

2. Your Consent

You always have the option to grant or withhold permission for us to process your information.

3. Usage & Processing

3.1 Transient Payloads (Processed in the Moment)

Glide operates with a modern cloud-based architecture to deliver high-agency capabilities. When you invoke commands or trigger workflows, payloads containing screenshots, active application contexts, and voice or text prompts are transiently transmitted through our secure backend to our AI providers. We do NOT store or persist your prompts, chat history, or screen contexts anywhere on our servers. Payloads are processed on-the-fly to return your response and are immediately discarded.

3.2 Authentication & Billing Data

We collect and retain limited data necessary for accounts and subscription management:

  • Google OAuth: We authenticate users via Google Sign-In. We store basic metadata associated with your Google account (email address and name) to verify access.
  • Stripe: We utilize Stripe for payment processing. Billing details are handled securely by Stripe in accordance with PCI standards.

3.3 Third-Party AI Providers

Glide relies on third-party AI partners (including Anthropic, OpenAI, and ElevenLabs) to power assistant features, voice outputs, and system actions. Payload contexts are securely proxied to these partners for immediate response generation. We do not sell your data under any circumstances.

3.4 Data Security

All transient payloads and retained credential metadata are encrypted during transit (using Transport Layer Security / TLS).

4. Retention of Information

We retain account and billing identifiers for as long as necessary to provide you with Glide's services. All personally identifiable information will be deleted upon account deletion.

5. Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR regulations, you are entitled to access, rectify, or erase your personally identifiable information at any time by contacting support@glide.inc.
