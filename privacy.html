Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 July 2026

At Glide, we prioritize your privacy and the protection of your data. We are committed to safeguarding all data collected, both personal and non-personal, adhering to the highest global standards of data protection. Our policies are designed to keep your personal data under your control while maintaining compliance with relevant frameworks, including GDPR.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Glide website and application service, governing the use of services provided by Glide to its users.

1. Notice

Whenever we collect information that could be used to personally identify you, we will explicitly ask for your provision and permission. This policy ensures that you are fully informed and consenting to the collection of your personal data.

2. Your Consent

You always have the option to grant or withhold permission for us to process your information.

3. Usage & Processing

3.1 Transient Payloads (Processed in the Moment)

Glide operates with a modern cloud-based architecture to deliver high-agency capabilities. When you invoke commands or trigger workflows, payloads containing screenshots, active application contexts, and voice or text prompts are transiently transmitted through our secure backend to our AI providers. We do NOT store or persist your prompts, chat history, or screen contexts anywhere on our servers. Payloads are processed on-the-fly to return your response and are immediately discarded.

3.2 Authentication & Billing Data

We collect and retain limited data necessary for accounts and subscription management:

  • Google OAuth: We authenticate users via Google Sign-In. We store basic metadata associated with your Google account (email address and name) to verify access.
  • Paddle: Paddle acts as our merchant of record and handles all payments. Your card details go straight to Paddle and are never seen or stored by us. We keep only a subscription identifier so we know which plan your account is on.

3.3 Third-Party AI Providers

Glide relies on third-party AI partners (including Anthropic, OpenAI, and ElevenLabs) to power assistant features, voice outputs, and system actions. Payload contexts are securely proxied to these partners for immediate response generation. We do not sell your data under any circumstances.

3.4 Crash & Error Reporting

When Glide crashes or hits an unexpected error, a diagnostic report is sent to Sentry, our error-tracking processor, so we can find and fix the fault. A report carries the technical detail of the failure only: the error itself, where in the code it happened, and the Glide version, build and operating system it happened on.

Reports are configured to exclude personal identifiers: no name, email address, account identifier or IP address is attached. They also never carry what Glide was looking at or listening to — no screen contents, no prompts, no voice transcripts, no file or document names, and no addresses of pages you had open. Server-side reports likewise exclude request contents, headers and credentials.

3.5 Data Security

All transient payloads and retained credential metadata are encrypted during transit (using Transport Layer Security / TLS).

4. Retention of Information

We retain account and billing identifiers for as long as necessary to provide you with Glide's services. All personally identifiable information will be deleted upon account deletion.

5. Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR regulations, you are entitled to access, rectify, or erase your personally identifiable information at any time by contacting hello@justglide.app.

By downloading, signing in to, or using Glide, you are agreeing to these terms. If you do not agree with them, please do not use Glide.

Anything that leaves your machine, such as sending, posting, sharing, inviting, or deleting, is shown to you for approval first. Read what it is about to do before you approve it. Work that stays private to you, like writing a note or drafting something you have not sent, happens without asking.

You can connect Glide to services like Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, and others. Those accounts remain yours and remain governed by those companies' own terms. 6. Glide can be wrong

Glide is built on AI models, and AI models make mistakes. They misread screens, they miss context, and they can state something incorrect with complete confidence. Check anything that matters before you act on it. Glide is not a substitute for professional legal, medical, financial, or tax advice, and you should not treat it as one.

7. Plans and billing

Glide has a free plan and paid plans. Paid plans renew automatically, monthly or yearly depending on what you picked, until you cancel. Prices are in US dollars and any sales tax or VAT is calculated at checkout.

Paddle is our merchant of record, so Paddle takes the payment, handles the tax, and appears on your statement. You can cancel any time from your account, and cancelling stops the next charge while leaving you access until the end of the period you already paid for.

If a price changes we will tell you before it affects you. For refunds, see our Refund Policy.

8. Fair use of paid plans

Paid plans include generous monthly allowances rather than genuinely infinite usage, because every request costs us real money to run. The current allowances are shown in the app. If your usage looks automated or far outside normal personal use, we will contact you first rather than quietly cutting you off.

The short version: if Glide is not working out for you, email us within 14 days of paying and we will refund you in full. No forms, no exit survey, no talking you out of it.

1. The 14 day window

Every paid plan, monthly or yearly, comes with a 14 day full refund. It covers your first payment on a plan, and you do not need a reason. Not clicking for you, not what you expected, bought it by accident on a Friday night: all fine.

If you live somewhere that gives you a longer cancellation right by law, that right applies instead. See section 8.

2. Try the free plan first

You do not have to pay to find out whether Glide suits you. The free plan needs no card and lets you use the assistant properly. We would rather you spend a week on free than a refund request on day three.

3. How to ask

Email hello@justglide.app from the address on your account and say you would like a refund. That is the whole process.

  • We reply within 2 business days.
  • Once approved, the money goes back to your original payment method.
  • Your bank usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it, which is their timing rather than ours.

4. Cancelling and refunding are different things

Cancelling stops the next charge. You keep everything you paid for until the end of the current period, then drop to the free plan. You can cancel any time from your account, and we do not hide the button.

Refunding gives you your money back. Outside the 14 day window we do not usually refund time that has already run, but read the next section, because renewals are the honest exception.

5. Renewals that catch you by surprise

Nobody should pay for another year of something they forgot they had. We email you before a yearly plan renews. If a renewal still takes you by surprise and you have barely used Glide since it charged you, tell us within 14 days and we will refund it. We would rather give the money back than keep a customer who does not want to be one.

6. Who actually takes the payment

Paddle is our merchant of record. That means Paddle processes the payment, handles sales tax and VAT, and is the name that shows up on your bank statement rather than Glide. Refunds are issued through Paddle once we approve them, which is why the money lands back on the card you paid with.

7. When we might say no

We rarely refuse, but we will if someone is clearly working the system. That means repeatedly subscribing and refunding, fraudulent or disputed payments, or accounts closed for breaking our Terms of Service. If we decline, we will tell you why in plain words.

8. Your legal rights come first

Nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under consumer law where you live, including any statutory cooling off period. Where the law gives you more than this policy does, the law wins.

9. Still unsure

Ask before you buy. Email hello@justglide.app and we will tell you honestly whether Glide does the thing you are hoping it does.
