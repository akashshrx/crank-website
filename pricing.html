Billing, in plain terms

Currency
All prices are in US dollars. Sales tax or VAT is added at checkout based on where you are.
Who charges you
Paddle is our merchant of record. Paddle takes the payment, handles the tax, and is the name that appears on your bank statement rather than Glide.
Renewals
Paid plans renew automatically, monthly or yearly depending on what you picked, until you cancel. Yearly plans are billed once a year at the discounted rate shown above.
Cancelling
Cancel any time from your account. You keep everything you paid for until the end of the current period, then drop to the free plan.
Refunds
Any paid plan is fully refundable within 14 days. See the Refund Policy and the Terms of Service.
